Nigeria

Nigeria is a country located in West Africa, known for its rich cultural heritage, diverse traditions, beautiful landscapes, delicious traditional dishes, and warm hospitality. It shares borders with Niger to the north, Chad to the northeast, Cameroon to the east, and Benin to the west, while the Atlantic Ocean lies to its south. Its capital is Abuja, and its people are celebrated for their vibrant cultures, languages, festivals, and traditions. Nigeria is a beautiful country filled with diversity and opportunities, and I am proud to call it home.
